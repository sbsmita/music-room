# MusicRoom.Marimba

A physically modelled wooden marimba, C3 to C6 (37 bars: 22 naturals in front, 15 sharps behind and 22 mm higher), played
seated with two yarn mallets or bare fingertips. Spec: `Docs/12-new-instruments/marimba.md` (revision 2) against
`platform.md`. Lab: the marimba reference (Node). Renders of this code: `Builds/Listen/marimba/`. Screenshots:
`Builds/Shots/marimba/`.

**Phase A** (its own copy of the project) built the sound unit, the interaction, the mallets, the model, the data and the voice
lines with their tests. **Phase B** (a copy of the instruments base project) wired them into the platform: the
registry descriptor with the built footprint, `MarimbaModule` / `MarimbaInstrument`, the mallet tool provider, the unit's
partials, recording, Sunny, the lessons' accompaniment, the display. No shared file was edited: `CONTRACT-REQUESTS.md`
lists what the integrator applies (the planner's reference data, the shared tests that used the marimba as a stub).
`PHASE-B-NOTES.md` has the wiring, the footprint verdicts and the open checks for the headset.

## The sound in one paragraph

Each stroke is solved on the main thread in double precision (a Hertzian yarn head or fingertip pad against four bending
and two torsional modes of an undercut bar tuned 1:4:10, kick-drift-kick, 2-15 us), and the bars ring on the audio
thread as free modal banks with a quarter-wave tube under each bar solved with the fundamental as a coupled pair. It is
bit-exact with the lab when run with exact maths, and within 4e-6 with the engine's fused maths; the demo phrases null
at -111 to -113 dB against the Node renders. Damping (a resting hand, a mallet held down) swaps pole tables: no clicks.

## Interaction (spec 5; `MarimbaPlay`, `MarimbaMallets`, `MarimbaController`)

- **Pointers:** each held, tracked mallet's head (a 17 mm sphere, plane = bar top + 17 mm) and the five fingertips of each
  free hand (plane = bar top + 8 mm). A hand holding any tool plays with the tool, not its fingertips; the desktop mouse
  hand never plays.
- **Real Quest tracking:** the grab pinch (thumb-index OR Meta's index pinch) because the tip joints sit ~17 mm apart when
  the tips touch; tracking jumps (> max(15 cm, 14 m/s x dt) or after a low-confidence sample) never strike, while a real
  9 m/s stroke that skips samples (10 cm per sample) still plays at vel 1; arming needs two samples 20 mm above the
  plane, so 6 mm of lever jitter at a resting head never re-arms it.
- **Strokes:** `vel = clamp(((vy - v0) / (v1 - v0))^0.7, .05, 1)`, mallet v0 0.25 / v1 2.8 m/s, finger 0.12 / 1.5;
  vy is the faster of the crossing sample and the approach (the fastest two-sample downward speed in the last 50 ms).
- **Dead stroke** (off until Sunny's tip turns it on, off in lessons): after a sounding stroke the raw head stays within
  -25 .. +10 mm of the plane for 200 ms unbroken, then still. **Mallet rest:** a silent lowering, the same band for 150 ms.
  **Hand mute:** two or more fingertips of a free hand on the bar, slower than 0.3 m/s, for 120 ms; the tip that played
  the bar never counts until it lifts 20 mm (a tap-and-stay rings). **Roll:** 4 alternating strokes on one bar within
  200 ms each. **Index first:** a non-index tip waits up to 25 ms while the same hand's index descends over another bar.
- **Sunny's signals:** `marimba.pickup`, `.release`, `.roll`, `.deadStroke`, `.mute`, `.backRow` (first sharp),
  `.fingerOnly` (6 fingertip strokes in 20 s, no mallet ever held), `.offCentre` (6 of the last 10 natural strokes at
  u < .3 or > .7).
- **Take events:** `marimba` (Onset: midi, vel, pos = u along, u = c across, mode mallet | finger, kind = the mallet),
  `marimbaDamp` (Release: midi, mode hand | mallet, pos), `marimbaUndamp` (State: midi).
- **Guide anchors:** `tray.left`, `tray.right` (the mallet grips, 11 cm either side of the centre), `bar.<note>` (top
  centre), `bar.<note>.tip` (the nearest tip, u 0.04: a sharp's reachable, full-voiced spot), `bars.middle` (F4).
- **Desktop:** a click on a bar's PlaySurface collider plays it at vel 0.7 from the hit point; Shift+click mutes it.

(96 BPM, rolls on the six half notes). Public-domain tunes from `11-first-five/tools/lessons-src.mjs`.

## The model (`Visual/`, built by code; nothing glows)

Rosewood bars (one dynamic mesh, 192 vertices per bar with the crowned top, rounded edges, the undercut arch and the cord
holes; texture along the bar, a seeded offset, flip and tint per bar from a 1024 x 256 atlas), a dark walnut frame (end
boards, four rails under the node lines, turned legs, stretchers, brass posts and red cord), resonators in dark anodised
bronze with brass rims (the low natural tubes C3-B4 folded back under the sharps, the treble's short tubes on one
cosmetic line with stoppers), a felt-lined tray on the front rail, two rattan mallets with grey-green yarn heads.
5 draws and 18,132 triangles at the seat, 3 draws and 3,872 triangles on display. A struck bar dips on its cord
(1.6 mm x vel, 11 Hz spring) and rolls off-centre; a lesson target lifts 3 mm.

## Also found

- The spec's pack rule "same-target repeats >= 0.6 s" does not hold for `marimba-first` (C4 C4 at 0.375 s in the Ode's
  last bar); the platform's rule (>= 0.25 s) does, and wait mode freezes time anyway.
- The phase-0 `window-near` display spot (2.71, -0.80) put the frame's back 0.14 m into the window wall; the platform
  moved it to (2.30, -0.80) (the frame now ends at world x 2.92, the wall at 3.20).
- With the kit at Right the hi-hat cymbal would stand about 7 cm under the right hand's grip for the top octave: the
  descriptor's two air boxes (the hands' field) keep the marimba and the kit out of one set (`PHASE-B-NOTES.md` 3).
- At the seat the model is 18.1 k triangles, not the spec's estimate of 14 k (round folded elbows); the display version
  is 3.9 k (request R8 for the descriptor's numbers).
