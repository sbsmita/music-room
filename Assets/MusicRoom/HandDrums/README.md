# Hand drums (a pair of congas: conga + tumba)

The hand-drum instrument of VR Music Room, played with bare hands, seated: open tone, bass, slap, muted and closed tones,
heel-toe, rolls, flams, soft to loud. Spec: `Docs/12-new-instruments/handdrums.md` (v2) with the review items of
`12-new-instruments/critiques/handdrums-unaddressed.md`; platform: `12-new-instruments/BUILD-GUIDE.md`. Lab (Node
reference, tools, vectors): `Tools/SoundLab/handdrums/` (the engine and the pattern/render tools). Listening files:
`Builds/Listen/handdrums/`. Pictures: `Builds/Shots/handdrums/` (the model shots) and
`Builds/Shots/handdrums/phase-b/` (in the app).

Phase B (a copy of the instruments base project): the instrument is registered and wired
into every platform contract. `CONTRACT-REQUESTS.md` lists the shared-file changes with their exact code;
`PHASE-B-NOTES.md` what was done in phase B and what is left for the headset.

## The id block

`InstrumentKind` / `RigId` / `UnitId` 6, sources `HandDrumLo` (19, channel 0, the tumba) and `HandDrumHi` (20, channel 1,
the conga), `HandDrumsModule` Order 34, TickOrder 22, carousel 6, id `handdrums`, event types `handDrum`, `handDrumMute`.

## How a stroke is made (each frame, `HandDrumsInstrument.Tick`)

1. Each hand that is tracked, not the mouse hand, not holding a tool and not in the system gesture fills an
   `HdHandSample` from its `HandFrame`; the heads' world frames follow the rig (`HdHead.FromRig`) when it moved.
2. `HandDrumsInput.Step(Clock.Now, left, right, dt)` emits `Stroke`, `Rest` / `Lift` and `Relabel` events.
3. A stroke goes to the unit at once (`IHandDrumSound.Patch` per patch; a second stroke in the same frame keeps its
   spacing as a relative time), dents the head, marks the hand (`PlayStamp`), feeds the signals and is recorded:
   - **the mute relabel (review item 9.7):** an open tone or a slap is held back up to 135 ms (the stroke logic decides
     "muted" within 90 ms, when a hand comes to rest on it) and then recorded with `IRecorder.Add(ev, atAudioTime)` at
     the audio time it sounded, as `mute` / `closed` with `mode = "rested"` when the rest came. The rest itself is in the
     take as `handDrumMute`, so playback plays the stroke as it sounded and the recorded rest mutes it, exactly as live.
     Every other stroke is recorded at once.
4. Rests and lifts go to the unit, the dents and the take (`handDrumMute`, open false / true).
5. The signals (`HandDrumsSignals`) publish `InstrumentSignalEvent { HandDrums, "handdrums.<signal>" }` for Sunny.
6. Headset, drawn hands only: the palm lift per hand goes to `MetaHandVisual.SetPalmLift` (contract request R1; found by
   reflection, so the module works without it and wires itself when it is there). It runs in "Play here" and in "Play in my
   room" with the drawn hands (the default there); it is off only while the real hands show through the passthrough
   cut-out (`IHandLook.CutoutActive`, the menu's "Real hands"), since the hole must match the real hand.
7. Desktop (not presenting): a click on a head plays the canonical stroke where it landed (rho <= 0.8 bass, else the
   open tone) through its own `Layers.PlaySurface` colliders; the mouse hand's fingertips never play (conformance 16).

## Platform interfaces

| Interface | How |
|---|---|
| `IInstrument` | `Tick` above; `Hover` empty (no glows); `PlaySurfaceAt` over a head (rho <= 1.05, -5..+8 cm); `PlayedParts` the heads |
| `IStowable` | `SetStowed(true)`: every hand lifted on the unit, the dents flattened, the lesson print off, the display set swapped in (same three renderers and materials, static heads, 4.8 k triangles), the click targets off; `false`: the seat set back |
| `IInstrumentPlayback` | `Play(handDrum)`: the canonical stroke of the zone turned to the recorded theta and moved to the recorded rho, at the recorded u (a `mode = "rested"` mute plays as the open tone it was); `Play(handDrumMute)`: the canonical rest of its part, or the lift; a dent at the seat. `ReleaseAll`: every hand lifted. `CloseLane`: nothing (strokes ring, rests carry their own lifts) |
| `IGuideAnchors` | `conga`, `tumba`: each head's centre, up = the head normal x the head's radius (Sunny's presets read the rim radius from it: OpenTone lands at 0.7 r toward the player, CuppedSlap at 0.8 r) |
| `ILessonTargets` | targets `{kind: conga \| tumba, zone: any \| open \| bass \| slap \| heel \| toe, label, hand?}`; the landing disc on the head (centre for bass and heel, rho .75 near side for open, slap and any, rho .5 far side for the toe); `Matches` live strokes ("open" also takes "mute", "slap" also "closed"); `ShowTarget` the hand print; `Hint` "Bass on the left drum" |
| `IContactSurface` | each head: in the head frame, rho <= 1, the signed height along the tilted normal, `ContactUse.Both` |
| `Rig` | `Handles` = shells + hardware (filled once), `Zone` = the seat boxes' bounds + `GrabConstants.ZonePad` |

Not registered: `IHandToolProvider` (no tools), `IInstrumentSurface` (not TableTop: in mixed reality the drums stand on
the floor), `ILessonAccompany`.

## Known gaps

- The head dents cost up to 147 µs per frame on the editor's main thread in the worst case (both heads dented every frame
  plus a resting hand); nothing while the heads are still. On the device, write only the rings a dent reaches if needed.
- The heads are not drawn ringing (no visual vibration): only the dents move.
- Device checks are in `PHASE-B-NOTES.md` (T-HD-CPU, T-HD-LAT, T-HD-RATE, T-HD-CLASS, T-HD-JITTER, T-HD-REACH, T-HD-MR,
  T-HD-LISTEN).
