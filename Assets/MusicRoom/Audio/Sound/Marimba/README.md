# Audio/Sound/Marimba: the marimba sound unit (phase A)

Spec: `Docs/12-new-instruments/marimba.md` sections 2, 3, 6 (revision 2). Lab and Node reference:
the Node marimba lab (`marimba-engine.mjs`). Engine contract: `Docs/10-upgrade/ENGINE-API.md`.

A physically modelled wooden marimba, C3 to C6 (37 bars). The main thread solves each stroke (a yarn mallet or a fingertip
pad against the bar's modes, Hunt-Crossley contact, kick-drift-kick at 1-2x oversampling, double precision) and writes a
strike record: the radiated sound during the contact plus a soft wood knock, and the exact modal states when the mallet
leaves. The audio thread rings the bars as free MB4 modal banks on the engine's own kernels (`DspKernels.ModalFree`,
`AccResolve`, `ModalEndBlock`), injects the states sample-accurately, swaps pole tables for damping, and plays the
records onto each bar's one source (Lo C3-B3, Mid C4-B4, Hi C5-C6).

| `MarimbaDsp.cs` | Audio thread, Burst: `Render(UnitSlot*)` (what `UnitHooks.RenderMarimba` will call), `RenderFast` (FloatMode.Fast, the engine's) and `RenderStrict` (FloatMode.Default, bit-exact with the lab) |

| `MarimbaOffline.cs` | Phase A host: owns a state, a `UnitSlot` laid out as the engine does, a pending queue with the engine's `When` and `LateDrop` rules, and renders blocks through `MarimbaDsp`. Used by the tests, the renders and the perf test until the platform registers the role |

## Commands (`GraphCommand.Op`, the unit's own sub-ops)

| Op | Payload | Audio thread |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Strike | `Id` = strike slot (`counter & 31`), `Flags` = LateDrop | a player plays the record from `At`; an Inject action at `At + nh` |
| 2 Damp | `Id` = damp slot (`counter & 63`) | a Poles action at `At` (the record's 8 poles) |
| 3 Undamp | `N` = bar | an Undamp action at `At` (the stored undamped poles) |
| 4 Silence | - | every active bar fades over one block; players and actions cleared |

## Verified (EditMode, `Tests/EditMode/Audio/Marimba/`, 41 tests)

- **Bit-exact with the lab.** The C# contact solve reproduces every strike record the lab made, to the last bit (21 of
  21, the four TV2 hashes too). The render (Burst, FloatMode.Default) reproduces every block hash of TV4-TV7 (78 of 78)
  on the lab's own records, and with the C# solve feeding it. TV8: C6 rings out to exact zero at sample 26623; Reset
  clears every state. The engine's FloatMode.Fast stays within 3.8e-6 of strict (limit 1e-5).
- **The demo phrases** (render.mjs's scores, exported with their exact gains) render to
  `Builds/Listen/marimba/marimba-*.wav`: null against the Node renders -110.8 to -113.2 dB with Burst Fast; the
  strict render gives the Node files' 24-bit words, 100 % identical. Long-term third-octave spectra identical to 0.000 dB.
- **Behaviour:** loudness C4 vel .6 -21.71 LUFS (lab -21.70), Ode bar 1 -24.14 LUFS (piano -24.01: -0.13 LU); louder and
  shorter contact with speed; the fingertip 2.0-3.3 LU under a mallet on G3-F#5; no step at the handoff; no DC or
  low-frequency push; no subnormal state, every bar retires (24 retired, 13 capped, as the lab); the 60 s random workout
  peaks at 1.8345 (lab 1.835); the cap fade and Silence click-free; hand damp 56.6 dB in 150 ms; a dead stroke at -40 dB
  55 ms after the mute; rolls sustain; Share 0.4 cuts the steady Work 168 -> 70 lanes (-58 %); the opt-in boundary
  crossfade is constant-power and leaves every other bar bit-identical; zero allocation in Strike / Damp / Undamp /
  Silence and in the render.
- **Memory:** 162,688 B of state (159 KiB) in one allocation, 0 baked.

## Cost (`Tests/PlayMode/Marimba/MarimbaLoadTests.cs`, category Performance, editor on the M5 Pro, Burst Fast)

| Schedule (bench.mjs) | avg us | p99 us | max us | live lanes avg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| idle | 0.06 | 0.1 | 0.1 | 0 |
| one note (C4 mf) | 0.75 | 1.1 | 1.7 | 6.1 |
| lesson melody (3 notes/s) | 1.3 | 2.3 | 17.1 | 20.8 |
| two-bar roll, 24 strokes/s | 0.8 | 1.7 | 2.1 | 13.9 |
| glissandi, cap 24 (budget 1200) | 8.5 | 10.3 | 27.7 | 168.7 |
| glissandi, all 37 bars (budget 2048) | 12.9 | 15.7 | 31.6 | 255.6 |
| seated worst, 30 strokes/s random (budget 2048) | 7.1 | 9.8 | 24.0 | 138.9 |

Per 256-frame block (5.33 ms). Two bars with no action in a block render together (four MB4 groups in flight, each bar
into its own accumulator, bit-identical to the one-bar path): 2.5x faster than the first version (all bars 33 -> 12.9
us). The Quest 3S is about 4x slower than this Mac (burst.md 10.2): about 30 us average for the seated worst and 50 us
with every bar ringing, against the platform's 200 / 300 us caps and the spec's estimate of 20 / 32 us (device check M1).

Contact solve per strike (main thread, managed Mono in the editor), median / p90 / max: medium 11.8 / 18.0 / 37.8 us,
hard 6.2 / 7.6 / 11.8, soft 11.3 / 17.4 / 25.4, finger 11.6 / 15.4 / 36.5; **a fingertip at vel 0.15: 12.5 / 13.3 / 15.5
us** (review item 9). Times 4 for the Quest: about 50 us median, under check M2's 80 us; the maxima are a shared
machine's outliers (seven other editors were running).
