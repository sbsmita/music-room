# Audio / Sound / Handpan: the modelled handpan unit

The handpan's sound (Docs/12-new-instruments/handpan.md section 2): a live modal model of a 9-note D "Celtic
minor" handpan on the engine's MB4 kernels. 54 groups / 216 lanes: per tone field the three tuned modes 1 : 2 : 3 and
nine weak upper modes, the shell's nonlinear pump (bloom at ff), sympathetic lanes through the shell, a 32-mode shell
bank (the tak), the Helmholtz cavity (91 Hz). Contact force derivative drive (no DC, no low-frequency push), strike
position through the mode shapes, finger and palm damping. It is the Node reference `handpan-engine.mjs`,
implemented operation for operation.

## On the platform's graph (phase B)

- **Share and Work:** `RenderUnit` scales its budget by `u->Graph->Budget x u->Share` (1200 = full): below 0.6 x 1200 the
  sympathetic lanes rest, below 0.45 x 1200 also the nonlinear pump and the second upper group outside a strike (the
  reference's 0.4 is 0.45 here, so the governor's Share 0.4 reaches it). It writes `u->Work` (mode-samples). Measured
  (`HandpanPlatformUnitTests`, six strikes over 0.4 s): Share 0.4 = 42 % of the work at Share 1; the notes stay (level
  within 3 dB).
- **Idle skip:** silent and nothing due: the graph skips it after 8 blocks; the next strike lands on its sample.
- **Loudness parity:** the graph path is the reference port trimmed **3 dB** (`HandpanDsp.GraphTrim`, after the safety
  clip): the lab matched `OUT_GAIN` to the harp's single reference stroke (-25.8 LUFS), and the four-note mf phrase rang
  on to +3.6 LU over the piano's mf phrase (-24.15 LUFS); trimmed it is **-23.53 LUFS, +0.62 LU** (LoudnessParityTests,
  +-2 LU). `RenderDirect` and every vector / render stay untrimmed (bit-exact with Node).

## Two render paths, one sound

- **Burst (the device):** inside `AudioGraph.RenderBlock` (FloatMode.Fast). The groups that only reach the output (upper
  modes, shell, cavity, sympathetic lanes) are batched four at a time with their own inputs (`Driven4x`), the rest go
  through `DspKernels.ModalDriven / ModalFree / AccResolve / ModalEndBlock`. Within 1e-5 per sample of the reference
  (measured 0.2-2.9e-6 on every vector).
- **Exact (the EditMode vectors):** `Render` called from managed code with `State.Exact = 1` runs the reference's own
  group-by-group order with two-rounding kernels: **bit-exact with Node** (all FNV hashes of test-vectors.json match).
  Mono evaluates a float expression in double and rounds only at its end (measured, `HandpanFloatProbeTests`), so every
  product and sum the reference rounds is written `(float)(...)` (a no-op under Burst).

| Idle | 0.3 | 1.1 |

The project's rule of thumb (README: the Quest 3S is 3-4x slower than this editor) puts the worst case at about
65-85 us average and 110-145 us max per block, inside the platform's cap (0.15 ms average / 0.25 ms worst, platform.md
10.3). Before the batching the worst case was 32 us in the editor.
