# VR Music Room: demo video plan (Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026)

Target length: **2:50** (the rules say the video must be under 3 minutes, public on YouTube or Vimeo, real headset
footage, no AI-generated video, English, no brand names, logos or identifiable people other than entrants). The user
asked for 3-4 minutes; a cut over 3:00 risks disqualification, so this plan fills 2:50 and lists optional beats to swap
in, not add. Everything shown is this Unity app and nothing else.

Judging criteria to hit on screen (25 % each): Innovation & Creativity, Experience Design, Technical Implementation
(hands only, 60+ fps, FoV-aware UI, passthrough with purpose, gaze), Polish & Presentation. Special awards in reach:
First Five Minutes, Reason to Come Back, Boldest Original Concept.

## 2. Shot list and script

Record all footage with the headset's own recorder (section 3). Every shot is first-person from the seat unless noted.
"VO" is your voice-over, recorded afterwards over the footage. Keep the app's own sound under the VO at about -12 dB and
let it come up to full in beats 0, 3 and 5.

### Beat 0: cold open (0:00-0:08)

Shot A. Seated at the piano, fire ahead, snow falling in the room (SNOW on). Play the first phrase of Ode to Joy slowly
with both hands visible. No menu, no Sunny (poke Sunny away before you record, or record after the tour is done).
No VO. Fade in from black over 1 s.

### Beat 1: title and the idea (0:08-0:30)

Shot B. Turn the head slowly left to right: hand drums, marimba, harp, the kit, the bookshelf, the window with snow.
Title card (plain white text, lower third): **VR Music Room**, under it "A living room full of instruments. Hands only."

VO:
> "This is VR Music Room. A cosy living room at night with a piano, drums, a harp, a handpan, hand drums, a marimba
> and singing bowls. You play all of it with your bare hands. No controllers, nothing to learn first. Every model,
> texture and sound is generated by the app itself, so there is nothing to download and nothing to connect."

### Beat 2: the first launch (0:30-0:50)

Shot C. Fresh install (clear the app's data first so the start panel and the tour show). The start panel: poke
"Play here". Sunny's fast tour: Sunny flies to the SNOW switch, the palm menu opens, Move instruments, the Instruments
grid, REC and PLAY, Reset view, "Press GUIDE for a one-minute song." Record the whole 39 s, then speed-ramp it to about
15 s in the edit, letting two Sunny lines play at normal speed ("Hi, I'm Sunny! Let me show you around." and the
goodbye at GUIDE).

VO:
> "Your first minute is guided. Sunny, a small sun-faced robot, shows every control with a ghost hand: the switches on
> the side table, the palm menu, moving instruments, recording. Poke Sunny to skip. Forty seconds later you are
> playing."

### Beat 3: the instruments (0:50-1:25)

Six quick shots, 5-6 s each, music up. Cut on the beat of what is being played.

Shot D. Piano: a scale with the fingers, then a chord. Show the key letters and the coral middle C.
Shot E. Drums: pinch a stick off the snare, play snare and hi-hat, then a nod for the kick, a crash and a hand choke.
Shot F. Harp: one sweep across the strings, then a palm stopping them.
Shot G. Handpan: tap three notes, a flat-hand slap on the centre.
Shot H. Singing bowls: circle a fingertip round the rim until it sings.
Shot I. Marimba: a mallet run up the bars.

VO (over D-F):
> "Fingers stop on the keys and the heads instead of sinking in. The piano reads how fast you strike. Pick a stick up
> with a pinch, nod your head for the kick, hold a ringing cymbal to choke it. Sweep the harp, or rest a palm to
> silence it."

VO (over G-I):
> "The handpan, hand drums, marimba and bowls each answer the gesture a real one would: a slap, a mallet, a fingertip
> rubbed round the rim."

### Beat 4: lessons and the music book (1:25-1:45)

Shot J. Open the music book on the piano's rest, tap "Ode to Joy" in the contents. Pale blocks fall onto the keys; the
purple cue marks the next key; the book turns to the song and marks the note. Play five or six notes.
Shot K (2 s). The menu's Lessons list, 36 lessons.

VO:
> "Thirty-six lessons, all public domain tunes, easiest first. Blocks fall onto the key to press, and the music book on
> the piano turns to the page and marks the note, with the letter under it. Drum lessons put the target on the drum
> itself."

### Beat 5: make a song, then the show (1:45-2:15)

This is the heart of the video. Record it in one take.

Shot L. Press GUIDE on the tape deck. Sunny: "Let's make magic! Flip the SNOW switch." Flip it. "First a beat: press
LOOP, then tap the drums." Two bars of drums. "Last layer: press LOOP and play the piano." A few piano notes over the
beat. "Now press STOP." "Saved! Now press PLAY and watch the magic."
Shot M. PLAY: the instruments play themselves, pale ghost hands on the keys, stars rise from every note into a
constellation on the ceiling, the walls paint a vine with flowers, trees and birds, the aurora moves outside the window.
Look up at the ceiling, then at a wall, then back at the ghost hands. Let this breathe for 15 s with the music up.

In the edit, trim the recording passes so the whole beat is 30 s: keep each Sunny line, cut the middle of each 2-bar
pass.

VO (over L, between Sunny's lines, short):
> "Press GUIDE and Sunny builds a song with you in a minute: a drum loop, a piano layer, saved to Recordings."

VO (over M):
> "Then press PLAY. Your song plays itself back, and the room listens: ghost hands play it, every note
> becomes a star on the ceiling, the melody paints flowers and birds on the walls, and the sky outside answers. Every
> recording gets its own painting in the gallery. That is the reason to come back."

### Beat 6: your own room (2:15-2:35)

Shot N. From the start panel choose "Play in my room" (or the menu row). Passthrough: the piano lands on your real
table; your real hands play it. Keep real people, brand logos and screens out of frame (cover a TV or laptop).
Shot O. Flip FIRE on the side table: a hearth grows up against your real wall and the fire flares. Flip SNOW: snow
falls through the real room.
Shot P (3 s). Look at an instrument standing in the room: the amber halo. Pinch: it comes to you.

VO:
> "Or play in your own room. The piano sits on your real table, a fireplace lights up against your real wall, and it
> snows around you. Look at any instrument and pinch, and it comes to you."

### Beat 7: under the hood and close (2:35-2:50)

Shot Q. The overview of the room (recorded by moving the seat back with "Move instruments", or use the editor overview
screenshot `Builds/Shots/integration/overview_corner.png`; both are real app renders).
Three text lines, one at a time, bottom left:
- "Unity 6, hand tracking only, 90 Hz with a quality governor"
- "All models, textures and sounds generated at runtime. No assets, no network."
- "Mixed reality with the Scene model: tables, walls, floor, windows"
Final card: **VR Music Room** / "Sit down and play." Fade to black.

VO:
> "Built in Unity 6 for hand tracking only, at ninety hertz, with every model, texture and sound generated at runtime.
> Sit down and play."

## 3. Recording the footage on the headset

1. Install the release build (or the round 4 dev build) and run it once so textures and voices are warm.
2. Headset recording: open the universal menu (palm pinch or the system gesture), **Camera > Record Video**. The
   headset records what you see with the app's audio. Set it to the highest quality available in Settings > System >
   Camera (record at 1080p if offered, landscape).
   - If you prefer a cable: `adb shell screenrecord --size 1920x1080 /sdcard/take.mp4` records video only (no audio), so
     keep the headset recorder for the sound.
3. Hands: keep the room bright (hand tracking) and your hands inside the view when they play. The recording is one
   eye's view, so look at what you want the viewer to see; move the head slowly, no quick turns.
4. For beat 2 clear the app's data first (Settings > Apps > VR Music Room > Clear data, or `adb shell pm clear
   com.vrmusicroom.app`) so the start panel and the first-run tour appear.
5. For beat 6 do a Space Setup first so the piano finds the table and the hearth finds a wall. Clear the frame of
   brand logos, screens and other people before you start.
6. Record each beat as its own take, two or three attempts each. Pull them off with
   `adb pull /sdcard/Oculus/VideoShots/` (or the Meta Quest Developer Hub's Media panel).
7. Do not use any screen capture that shows the system UI, the universal menu or the boundary.

## 4. Voice-over and edit

- Record the VO in a quiet room on the phone or Mac, in one pass per beat, at a calm pace (the script above is about
  360 words: about 2:25 of speech, leaving the music its own moments). Your own voice: the rules forbid AI-generated video,
  so keep the narration human too.
- Any editor works (iMovie or DaVinci Resolve are free). 1920x1080, 30 fps, stereo. Title cards in a plain sans-serif,
  white on the footage with a soft dark gradient. No stock music: the app's own sound is the soundtrack.
- Levels: VO at -16 LUFS integrated, app audio ducked to about -12 dB under the VO and full between lines.
- Check before upload: under 3:00, no brand names or logos in frame or on cards, no other people, no footage from any
  other app or project, English only.
- Upload as a public YouTube video (not unlisted), title "VR Music Room", put the link on the Devpost form.

## 5. If a beat does not record well

- Beat 2 (tour) can be replaced by Menu > Sunny > "Show me around" on an existing install; it is the same tour.
- Beat 5 can be replaced by: LOOP, drums, LOOP, piano, STOP, PLAY by hand (no Sunny) with the same VO.
- Beat 6 needs passthrough; if no table is found the piano stays where you place it, which still reads fine.
- Optional swap-ins if a beat runs short: the Recordings list with its star thumbnails; "Move instruments" dragging the
  kit by a pinch; flicking paint at a wall; the gallery picture of a saved song.
